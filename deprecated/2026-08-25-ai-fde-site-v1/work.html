The projects differ, but the pattern is consistent: understand a difficult real-world problem, design the right system around the workflow and constraints, and carry it through evaluation, deployment, and iteration.

Enterprise AI

Customized agentic systems for complex operational workflows

Enterprise AI rarely starts with a clean prompt. The hard part is understanding the workflow, the proprietary knowledge and data it depends on, the existing systems it must work with, and where human expertise needs to remain in the loop.

What I work on

  • Work with internal customers, domain experts, architects, data scientists, data engineers, and software engineers to turn ambiguous operational problems into technical solutions.
  • Design agentic systems grounded in proprietary enterprise data and domain knowledge, with retrieval, tool use, structured workflows, and human oversight.
  • Build evaluation and feedback loops around real tasks rather than relying only on generic model benchmarks.
  • Carry systems through cloud deployment, integration with enterprise applications, user testing, and iterative production improvement.

Some enterprise work is intentionally described at a high level because it involves proprietary internal processes and systems.

Pre-launch

OneClick is a comprehensive practice-management platform for mental-health professionals. I co-founded the company and have worked across product discovery, technical architecture, full-stack software, cloud infrastructure, security and privacy, deployment, user experience, and customer development.
