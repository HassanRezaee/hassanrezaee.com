Founder work

OneClick

Practice-management software for mental-health professionals.

I co-founded OneClick after seeing how much administrative work still depends on disconnected tools, repetitive manual steps, and software that does not reflect how independent mental-health professionals actually work.

We have spent the past two years building the product in close collaboration with clinicians. My role spans customer discovery, product decisions, software architecture, engineering, cloud infrastructure, security, integrations, deployment, and production operations.

What we're building

OneClick brings everyday practice-management workflows into a single platform, with software automation and selected AI-driven capabilities focused on reducing administrative work.

The product is administrative software; it is not intended to replace professional clinical judgment.

Built with clinicians

Product decisions are shaped through ongoing conversations, testing, and co-design with practicing clinicians. Feedback influences workflow design, usability, feature priorities, automation, and the technical trade-offs behind the product.

Status

OneClick is currently preparing for launch.
