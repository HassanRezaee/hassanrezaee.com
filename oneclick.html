OneClick · Pre-launch

Building practice-management software around real clinician workflows.

OneClick is a comprehensive practice-management platform for mental-health professionals. We are building it closely with clinicians to reduce administrative friction, simplify everyday workflows, and introduce automation and AI where they provide meaningful value.

Pre-launch

Founder story

Why we built it

OneClick grew from seeing how much administrative work still depends on disconnected tools, repetitive manual steps, and software that does not reflect how independent mental-health professionals actually work. We built OneClick to bring those workflows into a more integrated system designed around the people who use it every day.

How it is built

Built with users, not just for them

Product decisions are shaped through ongoing conversations, testing, and co-design with clinicians. Feedback is translated into workflow changes, product priorities, usability improvements, automation, and technical trade-offs.

  • Customer discovery with practicing clinicians
  • Iterative co-design rather than assumption-driven roadmaps
  • End-to-end product engineering
  • Production cloud infrastructure
  • Security and privacy as design constraints
  • Reliable operations
  • Selected AI-driven features where they reduce administrative work

AI philosophy

AI where it helps, human control where it matters

OneClick's AI strategy is to reduce repetitive administrative work and unnecessary user actions. AI should support professional workflows, not replace clinical judgment. Important or consequential actions should remain subject to appropriate user review.
